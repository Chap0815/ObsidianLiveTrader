# Community conduct

Keep issues, discussions and reviews respectful, constructive and relevant.

- Critique code and evidence, not people.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate or publish personal information.
- Do not post scams, referral spam, impersonation or profit guarantees.
- Never pressure anyone to disclose keys, transfer funds or enable live trading.
- Respect the project license and the privacy of financial information.

The maintainer may remove harmful content, lock discussions or restrict
participation. Use GitHub's reporting tools for abuse; keep sensitive details
private. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
